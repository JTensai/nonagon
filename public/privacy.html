Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, Facebook or email, we receive your name, email address and an account ID from that service. We store the goals and steps you write so you can see them on any device.

How we use it

Only to sign you in and show you your own goals. We don't sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone. A goal is visible to others only if you turn on link sharing for it.

Where it's stored

Your account and goals are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore).

Deleting your data

Delete any goal from its menu with Delete goal. To delete your account and everything in it, email tricksfolsomllc@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, and we'll remove it within 30 days. If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Nonagon under Facebook Settings > Apps and Websites.
